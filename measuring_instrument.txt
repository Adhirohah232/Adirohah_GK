1. "Thermograph": self-recording thermometer
2. "Seismograph": measures and records Earthquakes.
3. "Psychrometer": measures humidity
4. "Planimeter": measures irregular plane area on maps.
5. "Hygrometer": Measures Humidity
6. "Fathometer": measures depth of the ocean.
7. "Clinometer": determines the difference in elevation between two points.
8. "Chronometer": time - keeping instrument
9. "Altimeter": measures height, used mainly in planes.
10. "Anemometer": Measures wind velocity.
11. "Barometer": measures pressure.
12. "Beaufort scale": identifies wind strength.
13. "Theodolite": measures angular distance in vertical plane and horizontal plane.
