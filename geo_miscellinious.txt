1. Great Indian desert/Thar desert is located on the western margin of which of the following mountain ranges?
[Vindya range, Aravalli range, Eastern ghats, satpura range]
Ans: Aravalli range


2. Steel city of India?
[Jamshedpur, Varanasi, Mumbai, Patna]
Ans: Jamshedpur


3. The longest river in India flows through which of the following states before draining into the Bay of Bengal?
[Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, West Bengal, Kerala]
Ans: West Bengal


4. Which of the following mountain passes connects Himachal Pradesh to Ladakh?
[Shipki La, Rohtang Pass, Nathu La, Bara Lacha La]
Ans: Bara Lacha La


5. Which state in India is the leading producer of coffee?
[Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh]
Ans: Karnataka
