1. "Erie Canal": USA; connects Lake Erie and Hudson River.
2. "New Waterway": Netherlands; connects Rotterdam to the North Sea.
3. "Volga-Don Canal" : Russia; connects the Volga River (Volgograd) and the Don River (Volgodonsk), between Rostov and Stalingrad.
4. "Welland Canal" : Canada; connects Lake Ontario and Lake Erie.
5. "K. P. (Kandelaru-Poondi) Canal" : India; connects Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.
6. "Suez Canal" : Egypt; connects the Mediterranean Sea to the Red Sea via the Isthmus of Suez.
7. "Panama Canal" : Panama; connects the Caribbean Sea (Atlantic Ocean) and the Pacific Ocean.
8. "Albert Canal" : Western Europe (Belgium); connects Antwerp with Liège, and the Meuse River with the Scheldt River also connects with the Canal Dessel-Turnhout-Schoten.
